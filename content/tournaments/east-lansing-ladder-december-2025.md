## Sign-up
The registration deadline is **Saturday, December 20, 10 PM EST.**
To play, you must register and pay online before the deadline because pairing for round one is performed prior to tournament start.
### Standard Entry
The standard entry is $19 for active United States Chess Federation (USCF) members, if paid before Friday, December 19, 10 PM EST. If payment is made after December 19, the entry is $24. **[Standard Registration Form](https://forms.gle/tYumUGgczKqeyA1P7)**

### Discounted USCF Membership
To activate/reactivate USCF membership at a discount, please follow the instructions in this form. **[Discounted Membership Form](https://forms.gle/SQg5vnswCxhQWfYy9)**

## Details 
- Date: Sunday, December 21, 2025.
- Scheduled Time: Runs from 1:15-4:35 PM ET (rounds at 1:15, 2:55)
- Location: [East Lansing Public Library - 950 Abbot Rd, East Lansing, MI 48823](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Qo6N48RPqqG8MSEs5)
- Logistics: **Fill out and follow the instructions in the registration form.** If you wish to refund your registration, please email [okemoschess24@gmail(DOT)com](mailto:okemoschess24@gmail.com?subject=REFUND%20%2D%20December%2021%2C%202025%20East%20Lansing%20Ladder) with a refund request, using your registration email, prior to the registration deadline (Saturday, December 20, 10 PM EST)
- Prizes: Anyone who wins twice wins $50.
- Format: modified ladder, players with same scores play each other in ladder format (to make all games competitive). See the following 6 player example with players numbered by rating.\
  Round I pairings: 1-2,4-3,5-6, 1 wins, 4-3 draw, 5 wins.\
  Round II pairings:  5-1 (both players with full point), 3-2, 6-4.\
  Note that no player is guarenteed to play both white and black.
- Rating: USCF quick and regular rated
- Time control: Game 50, no delay nor increment (G/50;d0)
- Please bring: chess clock, pen, and chess set
