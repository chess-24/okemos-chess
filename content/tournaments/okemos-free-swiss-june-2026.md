## Sign-up
The registration deadline is **Thursday, June 18, 10 PM EST.**
To play, you must register before the deadline because pairing for round one is performed prior to tournament start. **[Registration Form](https://forms.gle/f2BoUwqDCbJfDEh17)**

## Details
- Logistics: Fill out and follow the instructions in the registration form. Space is limited so act quick.
- Date: Saturday, June 20, 2026.
- Entry Fee: Free ($0)
- Scheduled Time: Runs from 1:30 PM - 6:30 PM ET (rounds at 1:30, 3, 4:30) All players play all 3 rounds regardless of score.
- Location: [Okemos Public Library - 4321 Okemos Rd, Okemos, MI 48864](https://www.google.com/maps/place/CADL+-+Okemos+Library/@42.7046959,-84.4327772,15.83z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x8822dd35d7c56e1b:0xfe6b2953ed30f433!8m2!3d42.7073755!4d-84.4329771!16s%2Fg%2F1hc11yyns?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDUzMS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
- Prizes: Any unrated (new player) who wins all 3 of their games wins free entry into our July 11th event. 
- Format: 3 round swiss, all players play 3 rounds, no elimination. Tournament director will section players off by rating to ensure competitive play.
- Rating mode: USCF regular and quick rated
- Time control: Every player has 45 minutes to make all their moves (G/45; d0).
- Please bring (if possible): chess clock, pen, and chess set
