## Sign-up
The registration deadline is **Friday, May 1, 10 PM EST.**
To play, you must register and pay online before the deadline because pairing for round one is performed prior to tournament start.

## Details
- Payment: Please pay the tournament entry to OkemosChess24(AT)gmail(DOT)com on [Cash App](https://cash.app/) and include a note with the player name and USCF ID in the payment. If you're opening a new Cash App account, you can use referral code 5DWL4F7 to get a $5 bonus in Cash App.
- Refunds: If you wish to refund your registration, please email [okemoschess24(AT)gmail(DOT)com](mailto:okemoschess24@gmail.com?subject=REFUND%20%2D%20May%202%2C%202026%20Grand%20Ledge%20Swiss) with a refund request, using your registration email, prior to the registration deadline (Friday, May 1, 10 PM EST).
- Logistics: Fill out and follow the instructions in the registration form. Then, pay the tournament entry fee via Cash App.
- Date: Saturday, May 2, 2026.
- Scheduled Time: Runs from 10:15 AM - 4 PM ET (rounds at 10:15, 11:15, 12:20, 1:50, 3:00, lunch from 1:20-1:50)
- Location: [Grand Ledge Area District Library - 131 E Jefferson St, Grand Ledge, MI 48837](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Grand+Ledge+Area+District+Library/@42.7530744,-84.7453808,257m/data=!3m2!1e3!5s0x882297db25723b97:0x130483a9f1383369!4m6!3m5!1s0x882297db4a74adbd:0xcc2ee1f3169b9feb!8m2!3d42.7526486!4d-84.745093!16s%2Fm%2F0j959vt?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDIyNS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
- Prizes: ($420 based on a target turnout of 35 players) 1st: $140, 2nd: $120, Top Under 1400: $100, Top Upset: $60. Note that these prizes scale with player turnout.
- Upsets: A game is considered an upset if a lower rated players defeats a higher rated player. We rank upsets based on the rating differential of the two players, with the upset featuring the largest differential winning the prize. 
- Ratings: We will take official USCF ratings on the evening of May 1st to be player ratings. If no such ratings are available, we will use provisional ratings. If that is unavailable, the player is considered unrated for the purposes of prizes. Unrated players may not win prizes other than 1st and 2nd place prizes nor do their defeats/victories count as upsets.
- Format: 5 round swiss, all players play 5 rounds, no elimination
- Rating mode: USCF regular and quick rated
- Time control: Game 30 with no delay nor increment (G/30; d0)
- Please bring: chess clock, pen, and chess set
